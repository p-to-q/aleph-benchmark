# Community conduct

We want technical disagreement to remain precise, respectful, and useful.
Participants must not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or expose private
information about another person. Critique claims, evidence, code, and methods;
do not attack people.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove abusive contributions and may limit
participation when needed to protect the community. Report sensitive conduct
issues through the private path described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). If a
private path is unavailable, open a minimal issue requesting one without
including personal or sensitive details.
